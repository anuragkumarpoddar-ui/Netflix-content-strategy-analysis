## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end data analysis of Netflix's global content catalog, covering both **Movies** and **TV Shows**. Using Python, the dataset was cleaned, explored, and visualized to uncover patterns across content type, country of origin, genre, audience rating, and release timing.

Each analysis is paired with clear observations and business insights, culminating in data-driven recommendations aimed at supporting Netflix's content strategy decisions. The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow — from raw data to business-ready conclusions — using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## 🎯 Business Objective

This project evaluates Netflix's content catalog to uncover how the platform's library has evolved across content type, geography, genre, audience rating, and release timing. By examining these patterns through structured data analysis, the study aims to surface actionable insights into Netflix's content strategy and offer evidence-based recommendations to guide future acquisition, production, and release decisions.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

1. What is the overall composition of Netflix's catalog — how many Movies versus TV Shows are there, and what proportion does each represent?
2. Which countries contribute the most content to Netflix, and how does this ranking change when titles produced through international collaborations are accounted for individually?
3. How has Netflix's content library grown over time, and are there specific years or months when content additions peak?
4. What are the most common content ratings on Netflix, and what does this suggest about the platform's primary target audience?
5. Which genres are most represented in Netflix's catalog, and what does this reveal about the platform's content focus?
6. What is the typical duration of Netflix Movies, and how many seasons do most TV Shows have?

## 📈 Key Findings

- Movies dominate the Netflix catalog, accounting for nearly **70%** of all content.
- The **United States** contributes the largest number of titles, followed by **India**.
- After splitting multi-country entries, countries like the **UK and Canada** contribute more content than initially observed.
- Netflix rapidly expanded its content library after **2015**, peaking around **2019**.
- Content additions peak during **July and December**, suggesting seasonal release strategies.
- **TV-MA** and **TV-14** are the most common content ratings, indicating a focus on mature/young-adult audiences.
- **International Movies, Dramas, and Comedies** are the most represented genres.
- The average movie duration is approximately **100 minutes**.
- Most TV Shows have only **one season**, with long-running series being relatively uncommon.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. Sustain investment in International Movies, Dramas, and Comedies while tracking shifts in audience preferences.
2. Expand content production in emerging markets to diversify the catalog and reach a wider global audience.
3. Adopt a more balanced, year-round release schedule instead of concentrating releases in a few peak months.
4. Increase investment in high-quality, family-friendly content to strengthen household subscriptions.
5. Continue prioritizing feature-length movies in the 90–100 minute range.
6. Invest in additional seasons for high-performing TV Shows to boost long-term engagement and retention.
